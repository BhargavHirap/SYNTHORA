# SYNTHORA Product Definition

## Category
Global synthetic population and behavioural scenario research.

## Initial user
A product, growth or insights lead at a small-to-mid-sized company who needs to explore hypotheses before investing in a real research study.

## Initial job to be done
"Help me identify plausible audience reactions, objections and useful follow-up research questions for this product concept."

## What v0.1 does
A single scenario creates a small set of transparent archetype-level signals and recommendations. If an OpenAI key is configured, a model adds qualitative interpretation. The numeric heuristic scores remain illustrative.

## What v0.1 does not do
- It does not represent a statistically sampled population.
- It does not predict actual purchases or election outcomes.
- It does not simulate an entire economy or society.
- It does not establish causal effects.
- It does not replace customer interviews, panels or field experiments.

## Evidence roadmap
1. Select one initial vertical and define a decision with a measurable outcome.
2. Acquire lawful, documented aggregate population data and record source/version/date.
3. Collect opt-in human research data with informed consent.
4. Define train/calibration and held-out evaluation sets before tuning.
5. Evaluate aggregate and segment-level errors, uncertainty and drift.
6. Publish limitations and avoid unsupported accuracy claims.
